# Requirements — Release and Kiroween submission

## Introduction

PLANRIFT is submitted to Kiroween 2026 (Frankenstein) before Friday 4 December 2026, 2:00 PM PT.
Every public or account action requires the user's explicit approval.

## Requirement 1 — Deployment

1. THE System SHALL be deployed to a public Vercel URL that serves `/` and `/demo` without login.
2. WHEN deployed, THE System SHALL pass the judge-flow e2e test against the public URL.

## Requirement 2 — Repository

1. THE System SHALL be published on public GitHub with all source, an MIT LICENSE detected in the
   About section, a README with install and test instructions, and the `/.kiro` directory.
2. THE System SHALL have a clean secret scan of the full history before publication.

## Requirement 3 — Media

1. THE System SHALL have screenshots captured from the deployed product only.
2. THE System SHALL have a demonstration video under three minutes, public on YouTube, showing
   the real product, with no copyrighted music.

## Requirement 4 — Devpost

1. THE System SHALL have a Devpost entry in the Frankenstein category with description, Kiro usage
   write-up, repo URL, app URL and video URL, reviewed and approved by the user before submission.
2. WHEN submitted, THE System SHALL have its submitted status verified on Devpost.
