## Description

```
PLANRIFT is a revision consequence studio for architects: move a door on a plan and see what the revision breaks before it is issued. Built with Kiro for the Kiroween 2026 hackathon (Frankenstein category).

Try it, no sign-in: https://planrift-omega.vercel.app
Studio: https://planrift-omega.vercel.app/demo
Code (MIT, .kiro included): https://github.com/AmirmLotfy/planrift

In the fictional Aster House Hotel sample, door D-101 moves from 1,700 mm to 3,000 mm. The engine finds that it strikes console F-07 after 15.7° and would push 188.7 mm into it. At 2,400 mm it clears by 391.5 mm. The floor plan, elevation and door schedule that show the door are flagged for review, the options are compared, and the accepted revision is recorded in a receipt.

Chapters
0:00 The horror story
0:23 A live model: computed, not typed
0:39 The approved plan and the hinge map
0:54 Revision A strikes at 15.7°
1:09 One scene, exact maths, Revision B
1:25 Drawings to reissue
1:40 Compare, review, accept
1:52 Under the hood: the Frankenstein
2:10 Built with Kiro: specs, steering, agents, hooks
2:34 Try it

All product footage was recorded from the live app, and every number on screen is computed by the engine. Narration and music were generated with AI tools; the sound effects were synthesised for this video.

PLANRIFT is a review aid for simplified room geometry. It does not certify code compliance or approve construction. The hotel project is fictional.

#Kiroween #Kiro #KiroweenHackathon26
```
