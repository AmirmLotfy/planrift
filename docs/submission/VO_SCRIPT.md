# PLANRIFT demo video — voiceover script (v2, 7 Nov 2026)

18 lines, about 330 words. Planned runtime **2:52** (hard limit 3:00). Every number below is what the
app computes; please read them exactly as written.

## How to deliver

- Record **one file per line**: `VO-01.wav` … `VO-18.wav`. Extra takes: `VO-05_b.wav`. I'll pick the best.
- Put them in `video/inbox/` in this project. That folder is git-ignored, so your voice never goes into
  the public repo.
- Also record **5 seconds of silence** in the same room: `room-tone.wav`. I'll use it to clean the noise.
- Leave about 1 second of silence before and after each line. Don't add music, effects or noise
  removal; I'll do the processing and the mix.
- Format: WAV, 48 kHz, 24-bit, mono (44.1 kHz / 16-bit is fine too).

**Recording your own voice:** use a small room with soft furnishings (a closet full of clothes works),
with the mic or phone 15–20 cm away, slightly to the side of your mouth.

**Using an AI voice:** paste the TTS version at the end of this file, one line per generation. Use a warm,
clear, mid-low "documentary narrator" voice. Suggested ElevenLabs settings: stability about 45, similarity
about 75, style about 15, speaker boost on.

## Overall direction

Calm, confident, and curious, like explaining something clever to a friend. Never sell. Pace is about 150
words a minute; let the numbers land. Start a little hushed (the "horror story"), get brighter from line 3,
go tense on line 8, relieved on line 10, and quietly proud at the end. `/` is a short pause, `//` a long one.

Pronunciation: **PLANRIFT** = PLAN-rift · **Kiro** = KEER-oh · **3D** = three-D · **CAD** = "kad" ·
**maths**: say "math" if you speak American English.
